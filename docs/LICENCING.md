# ALICE Licensing Philosophy

**Open Source at the Core. Freedom at the Edges.**

ALICE is an open-source platform built to empower humans and AI agents to work together in secure, isolated computing environments.

We believe open-source software and commercial innovation can thrive together.

### The ALICE Core

The ALICE core is licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0-only)**.

Anyone may use, study, modify, distribute, and commercially deploy ALICE under the terms of that license.

### The Extension Ecosystem

ALICE welcomes extensions, plugins, MCP servers, applications, and services developed by anyone.

Independent extensions may be free, commercial, open source, or proprietary, subject to applicable licensing obligations.

We encourage developers to build businesses around ALICE. Commercial success within the ecosystem is something to celebrate, not discourage.

### Our Commitment

- ALICE's core remains open source.
- Commercial use is explicitly welcome.
- Third-party developers retain ownership of their independently developed work.
- Public interfaces should support interoperability without requiring extensions to incorporate ALICE's internal code.
- Users should never be forced into proprietary services to use the core platform.
- The project should remain forkable, reproducibly buildable, and independently hostable.

**ALICE belongs in the open. What you build with it belongs to you, subject to the licenses of the components you use.**
